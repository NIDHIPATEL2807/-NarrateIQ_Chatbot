## Test 2 — Interview Mode (Personalization Test)

**Prompt:**
> Help me answer "Tell me about yourself" for a fresher data analyst role

**Bot Response:**

### 1️⃣ Build a 60-second "Tell me about yourself" (fresh-grad Data Analyst)

| Part | What to say | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Opening (5-7 sec) | "I'm [Your Name], a recent B.Tech graduate in Computer Science from [University]." | Sets the stage quickly – name, degree, school. |
| Academic focus (10-12 sec) | "During my final year I specialized in data analytics and statistics, completing courses in SQL, Python (pandas, NumPy), and data visualization with Tableau." | Shows the technical foundation the role needs. |
| Key project – Situation & Task (15-18 sec) | "For my capstone project, the university wanted to understand why student-event attendance had dropped. I was tasked with collecting, cleaning, and analyzing the attendance data from the past three years." | Gives a concrete problem that a data analyst would face. |
| Action (15-18 sec) | "I wrote Python scripts to extract data from CSV and MySQL, used pandas to handle missing values, performed exploratory analysis, and built an interactive Tableau dashboard that highlighted seasonal trends and demographic factors." | Highlights the tools, methods, and your hands-on work. |
| Result (8-10 sec) | "The insights helped the student council redesign event timing, increasing attendance by 12% in the next semester. My work was presented at the university's tech expo and earned a 'Best Analytical Solution' award." | Quantifies impact – recruiters love numbers. |
| Why this role (8-10 sec) | "I enjoyed turning raw data into actionable stories, and I'm excited to bring the same analytical mindset to [Company] as a Data Analyst, where I can help drive data-informed decisions." | Connects your story to the job you're applying for. |

**Putting it together (≈55-60 seconds)**

> "I'm Aditi Sharma, a recent B.Tech graduate in Computer Science from XYZ University. During my final year I specialized in data analytics, completing courses in SQL, Python (pandas, NumPy), and Tableau. For my capstone project, the university wanted to understand why student-event attendance had dropped, and I was tasked with collecting, cleaning, and analyzing three years of attendance data. I wrote Python scripts to extract data from MySQL, used pandas to handle missing values, performed exploratory analysis, and built an interactive Tableau dashboard that highlighted seasonal trends and demographic factors. The insights helped the student council redesign event timing, increasing attendance by 12% in the next semester, and my work won the 'Best Analytical Solution' award at the campus tech expo. I loved turning raw data into actionable stories, and I'm excited to bring that analytical mindset to [Company] as a Data Analyst, helping the team make data-informed decisions."

### 2️⃣ Structured Feedback

| What works well | What to tighten | Re-worded example |
|---|---|---|
| Clear intro (name, degree, school) / Specific technical skills (SQL, Python, Tableau) / Quantified result (12% increase) | Slightly long on the "action" details – merge "Python scripts" and "pandas cleaning" into one phrase. Reduce filler words to keep the pace snappy. | "I wrote Python scripts to pull data from MySQL, cleaned it with pandas, and visualized trends in Tableau, which helped the council redesign events and raise attendance by 12%." |

### 3️⃣ Quick Checklist Before the Interview
- ☐ Practice the story out loud 3-4 times; aim for 55-60 seconds.
- ☐ Smile and keep eye contact – the story is about you, not just numbers.
- ☐ Tailor the last line to the specific company (mention a product or data-driven initiative you admire).
- ☐ Have a one-sentence backup ready if they ask for more detail on the project.

## Test 3 — GD Mode

**Prompt:**
> GD topic: Social media does more harm than good — give me points to speak

**Bot Response:**

### Group-Discussion (GD) Coaching
**Topic: "Social media does more harm than good."**

#### 1️⃣ Arguments FOR the statement (4 strong points)

| # | Point | Brief supporting idea |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mental-health toll | Studies link heavy scrolling to anxiety, depression, and low self-esteem, especially among teens. |
| 2 | Spread of misinformation | False news travels faster than facts, shaping opinions on health, politics, and elections. |
| 3 | Privacy & data exploitation | Platforms harvest personal data and sell it to advertisers, often without clear consent. |
| 4 | Echo-chamber & polarization | Algorithms show us only content we agree with, deepening societal divides and reducing critical thinking. |

#### 2️⃣ Arguments AGAINST the statement (3 balanced points)

| # | Point | Brief supporting idea |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Connectivity & support | Families and friends stay in touch across distances; niche communities give voice to marginalized groups. |
| 2 | Education & awareness | Real-time updates spread emergency alerts, health tips, and learning resources to millions instantly. |
| 3 | Economic & career opportunities | Small businesses, freelancers, and job seekers use platforms for marketing, networking, and remote work. |

#### 3️⃣ Impactful opening line
> "While social media has undeniably reshaped how we connect, the evidence shows that its negative consequences — mental-health issues, misinformation, privacy breaches, and societal polarization — outweigh the benefits."

#### 4️⃣ Two-sentence summary / conclusion
> "In short, social media's capacity to harm individuals and societies — through psychological stress, fake news, data misuse, and echo chambers — far exceeds its role as a communication tool. To harness its potential responsibly, we must enforce stricter regulations, promote digital literacy, and encourage healthier usage habits."

#### 5️⃣ Communication tips for the GD

| Tip | How to apply |
|---|---|
| Listen first | Nod, make brief eye contact, and note key words before you jump in. |
| Build on others | Use phrases like "I agree with [Name] and would add that…" to show collaboration. |
| Intervene politely | If you need to redirect, say "May I add a different perspective here?" rather than cutting someone off. |
| Stay concise | Aim for 15-20 seconds per turn; deliver one clear point with a quick example. |
| Use facts | Mention a credible study or statistic (e.g., "A 2023 Pew Research report found 64% of adults feel social media harms mental health"). |

## Test 4 — Cold Email Mode

**Prompt:**
> Write a cold email to a recruiter at a product startup for a software engineer internship

**Bot Response:**

### Subject-Line Options
1. **Curiosity-driven:** "How a campus-project can accelerate [Company]'s next feature rollout"
2. **Direct:** "Application for Software Engineer Internship – [Your Name]"

### Cold-Email Draft (≈130 words)

```
Hi [Recruiter First Name],

I'm [Your Name], a final-year Computer Science student at [University]. I've built a 
full-stack web app that lets users visualise real-time IoT sensor data – a project that 
taught me React, Node.js, and Docker, and reduced data-latency by 30% in testing.

I'm impressed by how [Company] solves real user problems with lightweight, data-driven 
products. I'd love to bring my hands-on experience in building scalable front-ends and 
REST APIs to your engineering team as a summer intern.

Would you be open to a brief 15-minute call next week to discuss how I can contribute 
to your upcoming roadmap?

Thank you for your time. I've attached my résumé for your reference.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Phone | LinkedIn | Portfolio URL]
```

**Key elements**
- **Hook:** Quick mention of a relevant project and measurable impact.
- **Value pitch:** Aligns your skills (React, Node, Docker) with the startup's product focus.
- **Ask:** Specific request for a short call.
- **Sign-off:** Polite, includes contact links.

## Test 5 — Follow-up / Conversation Context Test

**Prompt:**
> Give me a stronger opening line for the same topic

**Bot Response:**

### Stronger opening line (replace the first sentence)

> "I recently built a real-time IoT dashboard that cut data latency by 30% — a capability I'm eager to bring to [Company] as you scale your next-generation product."

**Why it works**
- **Specific impact** (30% latency reduction) shows you deliver results, not just "I have experience."
- **Direct relevance** ("real-time dashboard") ties instantly to a product-startup's focus on fast, user-centric features.
- **Active intent** ("eager to bring… as you scale") signals you've researched the company's growth stage and are ready to contribute now.

Feel free to swap the project detail for another that matches the startup's tech stack (e.g., "a micro-service that handled 10k requests/sec") while keeping the same structure.
